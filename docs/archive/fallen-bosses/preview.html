The Fallen Bosses

The nightmare versions of the five bosses of worlds 1 to 5, one for each nightmare book, each drawn beside the boss as it is in the game today. Every one keeps its silhouette and its animations, so a player who beat the story boss knows exactly who has come back. Nothing here is in the game.

The Fallen Gravekeeper

Nightmare Pumpkin Patch

The eclipse came, and the Gravekeeper came back with it. He set the barn alight with his own lantern.

Burnt and returned: a charred robe with ember cracks climbing from a smouldering hem, a scorched skull with eclipse eyes, the black eclipsed moon burning behind his hood with crows crossing it, a charred coffin lid chained to his back with the padlock across his chest, a counting crow on the shovel, and his lantern burning black-and-orange eclipse fire. Ash falls and embers rise around him.

The Fallen Poltergeist

Nightmare Foggy Hollow

The mist turned green the night the Poltergeist fell, and it never turned back. Whatever it touches begins to rot.

Rotten and reaper-like: the sheet stained black-green and dripping, torn open on the chest to show ribs and a heart burning green ghostfire, a reaper cowl whose peak burns like a wick, a skull in the dark of the hood with red ember eyes, yellowed teeth, bone hands with black claws, rusted chains with burning skulls, black fog curling off it, and a scythe of black fog looming behind, edged in poison green.

The Fallen Hexwitch

Nightmare Witchwood

As she fell, the Hexwitch cast one last spell, and it drank every colour out of the wood. Only her window still burns red.

Drained like her wood: ash-grey skin with sunken cheeks, hair of dead roots, a charcoal hat split by cracks with a cobwebbed hole, a crown of dead thorns and a skull charm, a bone-white cloak rotting up from the hem, treant bark grown up the broom with a screaming knothole, a cracked cauldron brewing red. The only colour left is red: her eyes, the cracks on her face and the hex sigil bleeding through her dress.

The Fallen Count

Nightmare Crumbling Keep

The Count was never really beaten. He was only waiting for the storm.

Storm-touched: corpse-grey skin webbed with dark veins and a lightning-white streak in his hair, a rain-black cape torn into rags with the crimson lining showing, a blackened iron crown with one spike snapped and lightning jumping between the points, wings a fifth larger and ragged with lightning crawling along the bones, longer claws, blood tears, crimson light in his mouth, and a blood spear in his hand that he plants beside him during the trance.

The Fallen Twin Tides

Nightmare Drowned Marsh

Under a blood moon the sea turned red, and the Twin Tides rose from it changed.

Two different twins now. The Blood twin: crimson raw-flesh plates, shredded fins dripping blood, bone spikes with torn membranes, a crown of bone, red eyes with blood tears and a maw of needle teeth, in a crimson sea; its eyes flare red when it sings. The Blue twin: abyssal blue-black with cyan bioluminescent lines, frosted horns with icicles, longer glowing spines, icy tusks and frost crystals, in a dark navy sea; its eyes flare ice-blue and a whirlpool spins in its water when it opens one.
