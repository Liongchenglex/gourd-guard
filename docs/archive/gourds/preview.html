Gourds

Five gourds for the nightmare worlds, cute like the pumpkins they help. A gourd sits on the patch and powers up every pumpkin flicked from the 3×3 square around it. Each gourd has its own shape, its own colour and its own power. The powers are still to be decided. Tap a gourd to see it on the patch.

A real frame from the game: night 1-4 in the Pumpkin Patch, with the middle cell cleared and a gourd placed there at the game's own cell size. Its 3×3 square glows. The pumpkins around it show no face until they bunch, so the gourd's face makes it easy to spot.

Tap a gourd card above to swap which one stands in the patch.

  • The rule: each pumpkin flicked from inside the glowing 3×3 square gets the gourd's power.
  • The gourd stays put. It is never slid or flicked, so the player builds bunches next to it.
  • On an edge or corner the square is cut off by the patch, so a gourd there covers five or three cells instead of eight.
  • Shape tells them apart. Each gourd has a silhouette you can read at a glance, so it never looks like a pumpkin of the same colour.

These three points are design guesses for the owner to confirm. Only the 3×3 power rule is the owner's.
