Paint Pumpkin

An archive design for a future pumpkin: white, spattered with the other pumpkins' colours. Three looks, each shown in the patch without a face, lit in a bunch with a face and a cheeky paint dab, as a shop icon, and flying into a ghoul with a paint-drop trail and a paint-splat hit. Nothing here is in the game.

Ability ideas

  • Wet paint. A hit coats the monster in the Paint pumpkin's colour of the night, and the next pumpkin of that colour to hit it does double damage.
  • Repaint. When a Paint bunch launches, each pumpkin next to its column is repainted to match the biggest neighbouring bunch, helping build the next bunch.
  • Colour thief. It counts as any colour in the patch like Rainbow, but only for bunches of 4 or more, so it rewards bigger bunches.
