Paint Pumpkin

Built from your sketch: a few big, smooth paint patches (a large blob right of centre, one wrapping the lower-left edge, a band along the top-left and a small one top-right) and a paintbrush for a stem, its tip dipped in paint. Four palettes, each shown in the patch, lit, as a shop icon, flying into a ghoul with a paint trail and splat, and hidden in a mixed patch beside the real pumpkins. Archive only.
