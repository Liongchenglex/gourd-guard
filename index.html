How to play

1Monsters walk down the seven columns. Each column has its own wall. Monsters that reach it start eating it, and if any wall falls, the night is lost.
2Swipe a pumpkin to slide it until it hits the edge, a grave, or another pumpkin. If it is already touching a pumpkin that way, it pushes the whole line in front of it.
3When 3 or more of the same color touch, they light up. Flick a lit pumpkin upward, or tap it, and its whole bunch flies up, each pumpkin up its own column.
4A bunch of 5 or more always knocks monsters back one space (bosses are too heavy).
5Pumpkins sprout every few seconds (set the timing in the pause menu). Fallen monsters drop pumpkins: tap them to send them over the wall.
6Graves show up in later nights. They never move and block slides. Press and hold a pumpkin to smash it.
7New pumpkins unlock as you go. Level each one up in the shop, and once six are unlocked pick the 5 you want to bring.
