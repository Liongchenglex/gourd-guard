## Shared description (both stores; under 4,000 characters)

```
The monsters are coming for your pumpkin patch. Slide, bunch and flick to hold the walls till dawn!

Gourd Guard is a Halloween puzzle with a twist: every pumpkin you line up becomes ammo. Slide pumpkins on your patch to make bunches of three or more of the same colour, then flick a bunch to launch every pumpkin straight up its column at the ghouls, mummies, bats and vampires chewing on your walls. Bigger bunches hit harder. Run out of room and the patch fills up; let a wall fall and a monster walks through.

SLIDE, BUNCH, FLICK
• Easy to learn with one finger: slide to match, swipe up to launch
• Every column is its own lane, so where you build a bunch matters
• Press and hold a pumpkin to crush it and make room

14 KINDS OF PUMPKIN
• Ice slows and freezes, Fire keeps burning, Grey pierces a whole column
• Black explodes, Deep Blue chains lightning, Pink repairs your walls
• Brown grows bigger the longer it waits, and Rainbow joins any bunch
• Level up your favourites and pick your team before every night

100 STORY NIGHTS IN FIVE STORYBOOKS
• Pumpkin Patch, Foggy Hollow, Witchwood, Crumbling Keep and Drowned Marsh
• Each world brings new tricks: drifting fog, howling wind, castle walls and the sea
• Face five bosses, from the Gravekeeper to the Vampire Count
• Three quests on every night to earn stars
• A bestiary in every storybook

ENDLESS NIGHT
• Opens halfway through the fifth storybook: how long can you hold out?

DRESS UP YOUR PATCH
• Costumes and skins for your pumpkins, each with its own hit effect
• Collect boss items and trade them for boss costumes

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Plays fully offline, no account needed
• Chunky toy-like art and spooky music

Free to play. Optional seed packs and optional reward videos; a short ad plays after longer Endless Night runs.
```
