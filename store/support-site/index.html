Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone. Gourd Guard has no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Restarting the phone or updating the app keeps it.
I bought seeds but didn't get them.
Close the game fully and open it again. If the seeds still aren't there, email us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple or Google. Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google in your Google Play order history.
A reward video didn't give me a seed.
A seed is given only when the video plays to the end. If no video was available, nothing is used up; try again a little later. You can watch up to 15 seed videos a day.
Can I turn off personalised ads?
Yes. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See the privacy policy for more.
Does the game need the internet?
No. You can play offline. Ads and seed packs need a connection.
