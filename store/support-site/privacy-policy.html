Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Gourd Guard game for iPhone and Android (the "App"), made by Lex ("we", "us"). In short: the App has no accounts and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and purchases are handled by Apple or Google with help from RevenueCat; those services collect some data as described below.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, settings, seeds, coins and items are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos and a short full-screen ad after an Endless Night run. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system and network information
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone, the App asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Seed packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email, card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases; it receives an anonymous ID created for the App, your purchase history and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Delete your data. Deleting the App removes everything saved on your phone. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use their privacy controls or email us and we'll help.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, and to object to personalised ads. Email us to ask.

Children

The App is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided data through the App, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Permissions

The App uses internet access only for ads and purchases, and (on iPhone) asks for tracking permission as described above. The game itself plays fully offline.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
